Pocket ESG Cheat Sheet

Quick 10-second reference cards for client calls and negotiations.

CSRD (Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive) Mandatory EU

Replaces NFRD and mandates standard European Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS). Affects ~50,000 EU companies + non-EU firms with significant EU turnover.

💡 Pitch Angle: "I deliver turnkey double materiality assessments and ESRS data collection frameworks."
Double Materiality Core Requirement

Companies must evaluate both Impact Materiality (how their business impacts the planet/people) and Financial Materiality (how climate risks impact their bottom line).

💡 Pitch Angle: "I facilitate stakeholder workshops and deliver the formal Double Materiality scoring matrix."
Scope 1, 2, and 3 Emissions GHG Protocol

Scope 1: Direct fuel/combustion. Scope 2: Purchased electricity & heating. Scope 3: Supply chain, logistics, employee travel, and product lifecycle (typically 80%+ of total).

💡 Pitch Angle: "I build clean, auditable Excel GHG accounting models using official emission factor databases."
EcoVadis Assessment Supplier Rating

Global supply chain sustainability rating across Environment, Labor & Human Rights, Ethics, and Sustainable Procurement. Enterprises mandate this for their suppliers.

💡 Pitch Angle: "I assist your team through documentation, evidence drafting, and target setting to achieve Silver/Gold medals."
